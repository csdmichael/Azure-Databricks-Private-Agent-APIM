What changes

Each completed deck releases about three hours of employee capacity. The largest savings come from data preparation, chart creation, and revision cycles.

Data preparation
The agent calls governed APIs instead of manual exports.
Analysis
Genie translates business questions into governed queries.
Presentation
The deck skill creates native slides and charts.
Review
Users validate insights and refine the narrative.

Where it applies

One reusable low-code pattern serves many business functions. Teams share the same governed connector and deck skill while changing prompts, data permissions, and templates.
